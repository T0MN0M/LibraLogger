About

Libra Logger — a budgeting app for UTD students.

Our mission

Libra Logger helps students keep their spending in balance. Log purchases or upload receipts, set a weekly or monthly budget, and get a heads-up before you go over — all in one place.

  • Track — log transactions, subscriptions, and refunds.
  • Budget — set a limit and an alert threshold.
  • Analyze — see where your money goes over time.
  • Learn — find UTD resources for saving money.
